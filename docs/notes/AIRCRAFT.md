- **Aircraft art (owner-approved approach):** the plane must look like the owner's art. Source picture: `docs/reference/super_mushshak_sprite_source.png` (plane on white). `python tools/make_aircraft_sprites.py` (needs Pillow) removes the white background and splits it into three aligned layers in `assets/sprites/`: `super_mushshak_body.png`, `_gear.png` (slides up behind the body to retract) and `_prop.png` (blurred blades, shown while the engine runs; a still blade is drawn when stopped). The tool prints the numbers for the `sprite_*` fields in the .tres. Flaps are a small code-drawn red line at the wing trailing edge.

## Air-to-air handling (owner 2026-11-21)
- `airCombat` on each jet in `src/data/aircraft.js` overrides its numbers **in the air-to-air mission only** (`Flight` merges it): thrust (power) and pitch rate / pitch acceleration (agility). Power (thrust/weight): Mirage V 1.00 < JF-17 1.07 < F-16A 1.12 < F-16D 1.19 = J-10C 1.19. Agility: F-16A/D and J-10C 35 deg/s, 360 deg/s^2 (lightest, most manoeuvrable, `stallMarginDeg` 11 like a fly-by-wire limiter); JF-17 30, 280; Mirage 23, 160 (sluggish, but has power).
- Measured: level acceleration 300 to 500 kt: Mirage 22.7 s, JF-17 19.8 s, F-16A 16.9 s, F-16D 14.7 s, J-10C 13.8 s; nose-up after a 2 s full pull at 420 kt: Mirage 44 deg, JF-17 59, F-16s and J-10C 68 (none stalls).

## 1965 war aircraft (owner 2026-11-22)
- **Era:** aircraft data field `era` ('modern' default, '1965'). Start screen: NAME -> **ERA** (1965 War / Modern day warfare) -> MISSION -> AIRCRAFT (only that era's jets) ...
- **F-86F Sabre** (Sargodha, FLT LT): no afterburner, ~420 kt top, slowest-accelerating fighter (0-300 kt ~50 s) but nimble (pitch 31). 6 x .50 cal, 2 AIM-9B, 2 x 500 lb bombs. **F-6 Farmer** (Mauripur, FLT LT; historically joined in 1966, owner: include it): afterburner, ~686 kt (just supersonic), 3 x NR-30 30 mm, 2 bombs, no missiles. **F-104A Starfighter** (Sargodha, SQN LDR): the fastest and strongest afterburner, ~810 kt / Mach 1.23 (the modern F-16A does 848), longest fighter sprite (owner 2026-11-22: made longer, display width 128 to 140; B-57 150), tail strikes come easily (`tailStrikeDeg` 10, others 15: lift off at 10 deg or less; the gear-down approach is ~8 deg nose up, so the flare has little room), least agile fighter (pitch 22), high landing speed (stall 165 kt); M61 20 mm, 2 AIM-9B, 2 bombs. **B-57B Canberra** (Peshawar = the Kamra airfield, SQN LDR): bomber, ferry + air to ground only, 6 x 500 lb bombs, no gun, still the slowest (~400 kt, just under the F-86) and least agile, but livelier since owner 2026-11-22 ("way too sluggish": thrust 60 to 64 kN, drag 1.5 to 1.35, pitch 16 to 22, spool 6 to 4.5 s).

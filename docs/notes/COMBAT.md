## Air-to-ground mission (GameState.Mission.STRIKE)
- **Start screen:** new MISSION step after the name: FERRY FLIGHT (base to base, as before) or AIR TO GROUND. In air to ground only `AircraftData.strike_capable` aircraft can be picked (owner: **jets only**: Mirage V, F-16A, F-16D; the Mushshak card is greyed out) and the ROUTE step is skipped.
- **World:** take off from Risalpur. Only the departure and one landing base exist (owner: **random real base**, picked from `GameState.DESTINATIONS`: Masroor or Mushaf). 2-3 ground targets (random kinds: bunker, radar trailer, missile launcher, fuel tanks, armoured vehicle; stylised, unmarked) stand from ~4.5 km out, 0.7-1.3 km apart (`StrikeMission` exports). The landing base stands `recovery_gap_m` (3 km) past the last target and is **hidden** (not drawn, not a runway, not on the radar, no landmarks) until the attack is over: all targets destroyed, both bombs gone off, or the plane `pass_margin_m` past the last target. Then it appears and the instructor gives its name and distance; landing works as on a ferry flight (WRONG BASE if back at Risalpur). Scenery blends from Risalpur's look to the landing base's look across the target area.
- **Bombs:** `bomb_count` (2) free-fall bombs drawn under the jet at `bomb_hardpoints` (texture px). **Space** (`drop_bomb`) drops one per press, airborne only. A released bomb keeps the jet's velocity (+ a small push down), falls with gravity and `bomb_drag`, and goes off on the ground (big explosion, smoke). A live target within `bomb_blast_radius_m` (18 m) of its edge is destroyed (blackened wreck).
- **Guided vs unguided (owner, 2026-11-18):** F-16A/D carry **guided** bombs (`bomb_guided`): the **BOMB SIGHT** and the ground marker, and each bomb steers its forward speed (up to `bomb_guidance_accel` 15 m/s^2) onto the live target nearest its impact point if that target is within `bomb_guidance_reach_m` (250 m). The **Mirage V** carries **unguided** bombs: no sight, no ground marker, each bomb scatters by up to `bomb_scatter_ms` (2.5 m/s, ~15 m on the ground), and a simple **RELEASE CUE** instead. Start-screen cards in air to ground read "2 x GUIDED BOMBS" / "2 x UNGUIDED BOMBS" (Mushshak: "NO BOMBS", greyed out).
- **Why an aid at all:** in side view the impact point is far ahead of the jet and off screen (160 m wide view; at 170 m/s from 260 m a bomb lands ~1.1 km ahead), and the camera only shows the ground below ~135 ft. The aids use the same fall model as the bombs (`Bomb.predict_impact`, within ~3 m of the real fall).
- **BOMB SIGHT** (`scenes/ui/bomb_sight.gd`, guided only): shown **in the radar's place** (top centre) during the target run (airborne, bombs left or falling, landing base not yet shown); the radar comes back after it (owner: keep the bottom of the screen clear to see the explosions). +-900 m of ground around the predicted impact point: cross = impact point, bracket = guidance reach, red blocks = targets (arrows when out of range), white bars = where falling bombs will hit, "IN RANGE - DROP!" when a target is inside the bracket, TGT distance and bombs left.
- **RELEASE CUE** (`scenes/ui/release_cue.gd`, unguided only): fixed box just right of the aircraft (HUD `release_cue_rect`), from 8 s before release: "DROP IN x.x" countdown, a bar whose marker slides toward a green window sized to the real hit window at that speed (+-(blast radius + half target width) / ground speed), flashing "DROP!" in the window, "LATE" after it.
- A target a falling bomb is already going to hit is skipped by the sight and the cue (they move on to the next target), so both bombs don't go on one target by accident.
- **HUD:** radar shows target blips (dim x when destroyed), bomb icons, "TGT x.x KM" to the next target until the base appears. Instructor: briefing, "targets ahead", bomb away, hit / miss by N m, all destroyed, landing base call. Result screen adds "Targets destroyed: X of N".
- Code: `scenes/flight/strike_mission.gd` (plan, targets, bombs, hits, reveal, marker, sounds), `scenes/world/bomb.gd`, `scenes/world/ground_target.gd`, `scenes/ui/bomb_sight.gd`; flight.gd builds the strike world and hides/reveals the base.
- **Attack-run camera (owner, 2026-11-18):** in air to ground the camera zooms out to `attack_zoom` (0.4) once the jet is `attack_zoom_from_m` (25 m) up after take-off, and back to 1 once the landing base has appeared, no bomb is falling and the last explosion is `explosion_watch_s` (3 s) old. Look-ahead x `attack_look_ahead` (0.35) keeps the jet nearer the middle so the ground behind it (where bombs land when flying low: a bomb keeps nearly the jet's speed and lands just behind it) is in view. Camera framing (follow_camera.gd) is in screen px, so at 0.4 the ground stays in view up to ~370 ft; the instructor asks for 300 ft. Falling bombs drawn x`bomb_draw_scale` (1.6), bomb explosions x`explosion_scale` (2); target and impact markers keep a constant on-screen size. Parallax bands and landmarks get their own zoom k = lerp(1, zoom, factor.x) (far layers barely change, near ones shrink) and stay seamless; at zoom 1 they are exactly the old parallax. Radar phase reads ATTACK during the run.
- **Attack-run camera, JS fix (owner 2026-11-19: climbing/diving didn't show):** the port set zoom = 42 / altitude and pinned the ground in frame, so the jet stayed at one screen height whatever it did. Now (`ATTACK_CAM` in `src/render/renderer.js`): fixed zoom 0.4 (Godot's `attack_zoom`), normal follow camera: low down the ground stays at 74% of the screen height and the jet moves up and down on screen; above ~125 m (~400 ft, 720p) the camera follows the jet and the ground leaves the frame. Look-ahead and vertical lead x0.5 during the run.
- **Weapons (owner's art, 2026-11-18):** each jet has its own weapon (`AircraftData.weapon_sprite`, `weapon_name`, `weapon_menu_label`), built by `tools/make_aircraft_sprites.py` (configs `agm88`, `gbu12`, `mk82`; sources `docs/reference/agm88_harm_source.png` (flipped: art points left), `gbu12_source.png`, `mk82_source.png` (its "Mk 82" caption erased)), drawn at aircraft texture scale (~2x true size like the jets; texture width = length in texture px: HARM 128, GBU-12 100, Mk 82 68), top of the sprite at the hardpoint.
  - **F-16D: AGM-88 HARM** ("GUIDED AIR-TO-GROUND MISSILE", `weapon_is_missile`). `scenes/world/missile.gd` (extends Bomb): drops clear for `missile_drop_s` (0.35 s), then the rocket motor burns `missile_burn_s` (8 s; flame out of the back, smoke trail) accelerating at `missile_accel` to `missile_speed_ms` (420 m/s), steering (`missile_turn_deg_s` 35) at a point above the target (`missile_loft` x distance, max `missile_loft_max_m` 250 m) so it climbs a little and then dives on. Locks the nearest live target `missile_range_min_m`-`missile_range_max_m` (0.5-6 km) ahead at launch; fired with none in range it flies on unguided. Bomb sight in missile mode: ground from the jet to past max range, jet symbol, range bracket, "IN RANGE - FIRE!". Instructor: "MAGNUM! AGM-88 HARM away!". Note: it hits kilometres ahead, so the explosion is usually only seen as the wreck/smoke when the jet flies over; lower `missile_range_max_m` to see more impacts.
  - **F-16A: GBU-12 Paveway II** ("GUIDED AIR-TO-GROUND BOMB"; name is my reading of the owner's art), the guided bomb as before.
  - **JF-17: Ra'ad (Hatf-VIII) air-launched cruise missile** (owner, 2026-11-19; art `docs/reference/raad_source.png`, sprite tool config `raad`, 4.85 m, display width 37): "CRUISE MISSILE", two carried (near/far wing hardpoints). Same SAFE / SEEKING / LOCKED rules as the HARM (`missile` block in the JF-17 data overrides the shared one): lock 1.5-8 km after 2 s, drops clear for 0.9 s, then the turbojet runs (small exhaust glow `flame` 0.4, light trail `trail` 0.25) and it flies at 240 m/s, holds `cruiseAltM` 60 m and dives onto the target in the last `diveM` 900 m (cruise mode in `stepWeapons`). JF-17 targets start at 10 km (`strikeAreaStartM`). Headless check: both Ra'ads hit in every run. The two missiles under the wing are part of the JF-17 art and stay drawn.
  - **Mirage V: Mk 82** ("UNGUIDED BOMB"), the unguided bomb as before.
  - **HUD weapon panel** (strike missions, under the name badge, `hud.weapon_panel_pos`): "WPN  <weapon>  x<left>". The bomb sight's title is the weapon name.

## Missile lock (JS, owner fix 2026-11-19)
- Problem: the first target stood 4.5 km out and the HARM's range was 0.5-6 km, so the JS port showed LOCKED on the runway (Godot had the same numbers but only showed the sight once airborne).
- Now (`src/sim/flight.js`: `missileArmed`, `stepSeeker`, `missileState`): the HARM is **SAFE** on the ground and while the gear is not fully up (instructor shouts once if targets are in range with the gear down). Armed, the seeker takes the nearest unclaimed live target `missile.rangeMin`-`rangeMax` ahead (0.5-**4** km, was 6) and must stay on it `missile.lockS` (1.5 s): **SEEKING** (blinking box on the sight), then **LOCKED** (solid yellow box, "LOCKED - FIRE!", LOCK diamond in the world). Fired without a lock it flies on unguided.
- F-16D targets start further out: `strikeAreaStartM` 8000 (other jets 4500), so the first lock comes ~20 s after gear-up, not right after take-off. Headless check 2026-11-19: SAFE until gear up, first SEEKING ~19 s later, both HARMs hit.
- Weapon card states: SAFE · GEAR UP TO ARM / NO TARGET IN RANGE / SEEKING… / LOCKED x.x KM · SPACE / EMPTY.
